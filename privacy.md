## Privacy Policy for FileHub

**Effective Date: April 17, 2026**

FileHub ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the **FileHub** mobile application.

### 1. Our Privacy Philosophy (Pure & Private)
FileHub is designed as a **completely clean, offline-first** utility. 
* **No Ads:** We do not display third-party advertisements.
* **No Tracking:** We do not use analytics, trackers, or cookies to monitor your behavior.
* **No Cloud Uploads:** Your files remain on your device and within your local network. We do not operate servers that store your personal files.

### 2. Information Collection and Use
We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. 

* **Local File Data:** FileHub accesses your device's storage to provide file management, fast searching, and previewing capabilities. This data is indexed **locally** on your device and is never transmitted to us.
* **Network Information:** To facilitate LAN (Local Area Network) file sharing, the app identifies your local IP address and connection status. This is used solely to discover and connect with other devices you explicitly choose.

### 3. Permissions We Request
To provide its core features, FileHub may request the following permissions:
* **All Files Access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE):** Required to search, preview, and manage files across your device's storage.
* **Local Network/Wi-Fi State:** Required to discover and transfer files to other devices on the same network.
* **Camera (Optional):** Used only if you choose to scan QR codes for quick device pairing.

### 4. Data Security
Since your data stays on your device or is transferred directly between your own devices via LAN, you maintain full control over your security. We recommend using secure, password-protected Wi-Fi networks when sharing files.

### 5. Third-Party Services
FileHub does not integrate any third-party SDKs (such as advertising, marketing, or data mining tools). We do not share, sell, or trade your information with any third parties.

### 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
